Assessment Framework & Important Notes

Please read before reviewing scores

The assessment, scores and commentary in this report represent my interpretation of each candidate's performance across CV review, first interview and (where applicable) structured competency interview. It is not a definitive assessment of their capability or potential.

Scores are based on specific evidence gathered through the process — each candidate's written application, and their responses to the questions asked at interview. Each may perform differently in person, under different conditions, or when responding to your own questions framed in a way that suits their communication style.

This report is intended as a guide to inform your own assessment at interview — not as a final verdict. The scores and commentary should prompt lines of enquiry, not close them down. The final assessment and hiring decision rests entirely with you and Lisa.

How scores are calculated

Each candidate is assessed across the ten dimensions of the agreed scoring framework, each weighted according to the priorities you set out. A score out of 10 is given per dimension, multiplied by the weighting to produce a weighted score. The total is out of 100. For Lauran and Maryse, where a dimension was tested at both first and competency interview, the final score is a straight average of the two — Lauran's Practical Fit & Logistics dimension was not re-tested at competency stage, so her first-interview score stands alone. Rachel's scores reflect her single, extensive first-stage conversation and follow-up meeting only, since no competency interview was conducted.
