Assessment Framework & Important Notes

Please read before reviewing scores

The assessment, scores and commentary in this report represent my interpretation of each candidate's performance through the process — CV, first interview and (for three of the four) a structured competency interview. It is not a definitive assessment of their capability or potential.

Scores are based on specific evidence gathered through the process. Every candidate may perform differently in person, under different conditions, or when responding to your and Lisa's own questions framed in a way that suits their communication style. This report is intended as a guide to inform your own assessment at interview — not as a final verdict. The final decision rests entirely with you and Lisa.

How scores are calculated

Each candidate is assessed across the same ten dimensions, weighted according to the priorities you set out. A score out of 10 is given per dimension, multiplied by the weighting, to produce a weighted score out of 100. Where a dimension was tested at both first and competency interview, the score shown is a straight average of the two; where only one interview tested a dimension, that single score stands.
