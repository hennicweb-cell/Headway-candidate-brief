About Headway Cardiff and South East Wales

A charity that has stayed
close to the people it serves.

Headway Cardiff and South East Wales began in 1985 as a support group at Rookwood specialist rehabilitation hospital, started by people with acquired brain injury (ABI), their families and their therapists. Forty-one years on, it's grown into a small, well-regarded local charity supporting 500–600 people a year across the Cardiff and Vale, Aneurin Bevan and Cwm Taf Morgannwg University Health Board areas — but it's stayed close to its roots. People with ABI still sit on the board.

The charity runs an Information & Outreach service, an Independence & Wellbeing day centre based at Llandough (plus satellite sessions in the community), a counselling service, monthly family and carer support, and weekly walking and wheeling groups that take the charity out to where people actually live. Funding comes mainly from spot-contracted centre places with local authorities and health boards, topped up by corporate sponsorship, donations and the charity's own fundraising — deliberately avoiding heavy reliance on time-limited grants.

"We develop incrementally, cautious never to jeopardise what we've already built, but ambitious to improve and grow to meet the needs of people with acquired brain injury as effectively as we can, as demand increases. We're small enough, and agile enough, that when something needs doing, or an opportunity arises, we can just get on and seize it." — Rebecca Pearce, CEO
The Role

Business & Finance Manager

This is a hands-on management role, not a desk-bound director's post. You'll own the back office — finance, payroll, budgets, systems and office administration — reporting directly to the CEO and working alongside the Director of Services. You'll line-manage two administrators (finance and people), and be based in our centre on the University Hospital, Llandough site.

💷

Finance & Payroll

The anchor of the role. Second-authorise every payment, monitor spend against budget, and run monthly payroll (Xero) including pension contributions and HMRC compliance. Prepare the annual budget, work with the external accountant and Treasurer on the annual accounts and trustees' report, and handle Companies House, Charity Commission returns and Gift Aid submissions.

📊

Grant & Budget Monitoring

Prepare forecast budgets for funded projects, track spend against them, and report back to funders accurately and on time.

💻

IT & Systems

Review the current IT support arrangement, negotiate a better deal, and make sure systems and cyber security are fit for purpose.

🗂️

Office & People Administration

Line-manage the finance and people administrators — supervision, annual reviews, expenses, recruitment — and keep office administration, health & safety and data protection ticking over.

🤝

Senior Cover

Become the third senior decision-maker on site, so the CEO and Director of Services aren't the only two people who can make the call when something happens.

About You

The profile that fits.

You don't need to tick every box on the JD — what matters is genuine confidence with financial detail and the ability to pick up new systems fast.

✓ Confident with numbers

Comfortable authorising payments, monitoring a budget against actuals and running payroll. You don't need to be a qualified accountant, but you do need excellent numeracy and a genuinely high level of accuracy.

✓ Excellent with everyday IT

Strong working knowledge of Word, Excel and Outlook, plus the confidence to get to grips with new systems quickly. Headway use Xero for accounts and payroll.

✓ Comfortable holding responsibility

Able to handle confidential information sensitively, work well under pressure, and make a call when something happens on site without needing to escalate everything.

✓ Not wedded to a sector

No charity background required. This suits someone from small-business, agency or SME operations management just as much as the voluntary sector.

✓ Mobile

Full driving licence and access to a car, as the base isn't easily reached by public transport.

✓ Good general education

GCSE English and Maths (or equivalent) as a baseline. Anything further is a bonus, not a requirement.

Bonus, not essential
  • Experience with Xero or a similar accounting platform
  • Line management experience
  • Experience negotiating or reviewing IT/procurement contracts
  • HR administration, health & safety or data protection (DPO) background
  • Welsh language skills
